# Selected Projects

## Topify AI

Topify AI is production AI search intelligence work. Bucky owned product experiences for realtime AI analysis, prompt intelligence, competitor analysis, reporting, onboarding, conversion, analytics, and agent actions. Public product: https://topify.ai/

## AI Job Intelligence Agent

The AI Job Intelligence Agent coordinates company research, job analysis, resume evidence, and interview strategy through a planner-led multi-agent workflow. Its system design includes triage, planning, specialist agents, structured outputs, tracing, evaluation, and human-reviewable results.

## RAG and Evaluation System

The RAG system follows a document, chunk, embedding, retrieval, generation, and evaluation pipeline. Retrieval and generation are evaluated separately using Precision@K, Recall@K, faithfulness, and answer relevance.

## MiniRAG Profile Assistant

This portfolio's MiniRAG assistant retrieves verified context from local profile documents, ranks chunks with embedding cosine similarity, and supplies only the selected context to the answer model. It is designed to avoid unsupported claims about Bucky's background.
